Written by the DSL course sync from templates/site/ in the toolkit; every sync rewrites it. {%- endcomment -%} {%- comment -%} The declared half of a schedule row's Details cell: the schedule.yml entry's own `details:`, whichever row type is showing it. Faculty prose, and RAW markdown - unlike `content`, which Jekyll has already rendered, front matter reaches a template exactly as it was typed, so without `markdownify` its asterisks show. Written once and included, rather than the same line copied into each row: every row type renders this cell, and copies of a cell are how one of them comes to render it differently. Takes `details=` rather than reading `include.event.details` itself, so the schedule layout's fallback row - which has an `event` of its own and no `include.event` - can use it too. The CALLER places it at the TOP of its cell, above whatever else that cell generates and outside every branch of it: a lecture row's links and its "not released yet" note are two arms of one `if`, and a blurb written into either arm would vanish on the other. The cell's own content answers "what is there to open"; this answers "what is this about", and a row wants both. {%- endcomment -%} {%- if include.details %}{{ include.details | markdownify }}{% endif -%}
