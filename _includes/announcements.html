Written by the DSL course sync from templates/site/ in the toolkit; every sync rewrites it. {%- endcomment -%} {%- comment -%} The Updates box announces content that has actually REACHED students: lecture and lab sessions whose materials shipped, assignments whose repos are handed out, and hand-written entries in _announcements. Display-only schedule rows (exams, special events, term dates) live in site.events and are deliberately left out - they are not news, and several carry no text to show. The release flags are what make that true. A session the plan names but whose files have not shipped is written with `unreleased: true`, and an assignment whose repos are not handed out with `handout_pending: true` (dsl_course/site.py) - and the WHOLE term is on the schedule from the day the plan is written. So an unfiltered box, sorted newest-first, announced the last week of term as "New Lab is up" before week one had happened. Filtered BEFORE the limit, not inside the loop: dropping a placeholder's bullet after the fact would leave the box showing one real update out of seven, or none at all. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign released_sessions = site.lectures | where_exp: "entry", "entry.unreleased != true" -%} {%- assign released_assignments = site.assignments | where_exp: "entry", "entry.handout_pending != true" -%} {%- assign all_events = released_sessions | concat: released_assignments -%} {%- assign all_events = all_events | concat: site.announcements -%} {%- assign all_events_sorted = all_events | sort: 'date' | reverse -%} {%- if all_events_sorted.size > 0 %}
